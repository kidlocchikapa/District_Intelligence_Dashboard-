# Zomba District Development Plan 2024-2028

## Strategic direction

The district prioritises equitable access to education, health, and social protection services while reducing the impact of flooding and transport barriers on rural communities. Investment decisions should be sequenced by service gap, exposure level, and the ability of the intervention to benefit multiple sectors at once.

## Priority actions

1. Strengthen rural service access by identifying TAs with the highest travel times to schools, clinics, and welfare entry points.
2. Focus on interventions that can be delivered within one planning cycle, such as outreach services, minor rehabilitation, and local referral coordination.
3. Reserve capital-intensive projects for locations where the population served and the cross-sector benefit justify the cost.
4. Align department plans so that school, health, welfare, and disaster actions use the same district and TA prioritisation logic.

## Guidance for planners

Planners should avoid treating every gap as a new-build problem. In many areas, a combination of outreach, route improvement, facility rehabilitation, and temporary service points can deliver faster results. Where flood exposure is high, continuity plans must be included in every proposal and every report section.

## Implementation notes

- Use TA-level evidence when deciding where to start.
- Document the evidence source for each recommendation.
- Track whether the intervention is preventive, corrective, or resilience-focused.
- Update the plan after each ETL refresh so that the latest metrics and case evidence remain visible.
