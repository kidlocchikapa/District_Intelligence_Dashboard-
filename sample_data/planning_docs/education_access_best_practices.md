# Education Access Best Practices

## Core idea

Education planning should focus on access, teacher load, and learner retention together. Building new infrastructure is not always the fastest solution; redistributing teachers, expanding classrooms, and improving transport or catchment arrangements can often reduce pressure more quickly.

## Recommended actions

- Target TAs with low schools-per-population coverage before expanding better-served areas.
- Use classroom additions when overcrowding is the main constraint and teacher numbers are already reasonable.
- Deploy teacher support and temporary classroom solutions where learner numbers are rising faster than staffing.
- Review out-of-school population estimates alongside school-age population counts to find the areas most likely to benefit from outreach.

## Best practice sequence

1. Confirm the TA with the strongest infrastructure gap.
2. Check whether overcrowding, staffing, or both are driving the issue.
3. Match the response to the dominant constraint.
4. Add continuity measures for flood-prone schools where seasonal disruption may interrupt learning.

## Reporting guidance

The planning narrative should clearly distinguish between short-term mitigation and long-term expansion. A TA may need both, but the report should state which action comes first and why. Cite school counts, teacher ratios, and learner pressure measures wherever possible.
