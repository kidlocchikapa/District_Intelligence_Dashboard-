# Social Welfare Targeting Note

## Objective

Targeting should identify households and communities most likely to benefit from coordinated support. The best interventions usually combine outreach, registration support, and links to health, education, and disaster response services.

## Recommended targeting approach

- Focus first on TAs where service access, poverty pressure, and vulnerability overlap.
- Use community outreach days to improve beneficiary registration and referral uptake.
- Align welfare support with school attendance, clinic access, and flood contingency planning where the same households are affected.
- Track who was reached, which referral was completed, and whether the intervention reduced exclusion.

## Operational guidance

1. Prioritise areas with weak access to multiple services, not only one sector.
2. Where budgets are limited, use targeted outreach and referral coordination before trying to expand every program at once.
3. Make sure records capture the TA, the household context, and the service link that justified support.

## Reporting guidance

When writing an analysis section, explain the vulnerability pattern, the welfare intervention chosen, and the source evidence used. The narrative should be specific enough that an auditor can understand why the chosen TA or community was prioritised.
