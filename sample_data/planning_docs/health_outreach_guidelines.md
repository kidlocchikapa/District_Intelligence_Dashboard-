# Health Outreach and Continuity Guidelines

## Objective

These guidelines support planners who need to improve health access in areas with long travel times, low facility coverage, or seasonal disruption. The preferred response is a phased package of outreach, referral routing, and facility support rather than a single large intervention.

## Recommended interventions

- Deploy mobile outreach to the most underserved TAs on a scheduled basis.
- Reopen or refurbish closed facilities where the catchment population is large enough to justify it.
- Improve the referral pathway between clinics, health centres, and hospitals so that severe cases can be escalated quickly.
- Prioritise nurse-led or community health worker coverage when the facility gap is too large for immediate construction.

## Planning criteria

1. Start with TAs where travel time is high and facility density is low.
2. Consider flood risk when selecting outreach routes and service days.
3. Use a mix of short-term and medium-term actions, especially where the budget cannot support a new facility immediately.
4. Track the number of people reached, the number of sessions delivered, and the reduction in unserved population after each cycle.

## Notes for reporting

Report sections should explain why the chosen intervention was prioritised, what evidence was used, and how the action will be measured. Avoid generic statements; connect each recommendation to a specific TA, facility gap, or travel-time constraint.
