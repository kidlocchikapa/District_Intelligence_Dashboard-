# Flood Contingency Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook helps planners prepare for seasonal flood exposure without waiting for a major emergency. It is intended for use in district planning, departmental coordination, and report drafting.

## Priority actions

- Map exposed settlements, schools, and health facilities before the rainy season.
- Define evacuation routes that remain passable when road access is interrupted.
- Identify temporary learning, care, and welfare service points for areas that may be cut off.
- Pre-position communication and referral steps for the most exposed TAs.

## Decision rules

1. If a TA has repeated flood exposure, treat continuity planning as mandatory.
2. If schools and health facilities are both exposed, coordinate the response across departments.
3. If the road network is weak, prioritise route resilience and local response points over distant service referrals.
4. If the exposed population is high, include evacuation and temporary service delivery in the same action package.

## Reporting guidance

Reports should explain the flood pathway, the exposed assets, and the contingency action selected. Use plain language that describes how the intervention reduces disruption, not just how it responds to damage after the fact.
